Automatic Irrigation System & Farming Assistant

A smart farming solution that automates irrigation based on soil moisture levels and environmental conditions. Designed to optimize water usage and support precision farming.

GitHub Repository
FARMING

Introduction

Project Overview

Objective

The project was developed to automate irrigation processes and reduce unnecessary water usage through smart sensor-based monitoring.

Problem Statement

Traditional irrigation methods often waste water and require continuous human monitoring, reducing farming efficiency.

Solution

The system detects soil moisture levels and automatically activates water pumps whenever irrigation is required.

Detailed Explanation

System Workflow

01

Soil Monitoring

Soil moisture sensors continuously monitor the moisture content present in the soil.

02

Data Processing

Arduino Uno processes sensor data and determines whether irrigation is necessary.

03

Automatic Irrigation

Water pumps are automatically activated whenever soil moisture falls below the predefined threshold.
